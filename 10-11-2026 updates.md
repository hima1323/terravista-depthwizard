# DepthWizard updates, 10 November 2026

## More disaster management from the same data
Seventeen additions to the application side. Nothing in the model changed: they all work on what a processed scene
already has (terrain, building blocks and heights, land cover, the flood scenario, the route finder).

- **All are what-ifs and planning estimates.** They are tested on synthetic scenes with a known answer, not on real
  events (`docs/CLAIMS.md`, row M11).
- **Ctrl K** finds each one (`sandbag`, `island`, `pump`, `boat`, `siren`, `hospital`, `rupees`, `cap`, …).
- **The situation report** has a section for each.
- **Glass UI only.** The classic viewer at `/classic` does not have them.

## Fixes found on the way
- **Evacuation plan:** two test buildings were reported as "no dry route" with the flood off. Their outline was ringed
  by roof pixels nobody can stand on. The shared "cells beside a building" function now steps over those, so the
  evacuation plan places everyone (0 unreachable, was 202 people).
- **Leaving a page with a tool on:** the tool is now marked off before it tidies up.

## Files

  `components/ToolCards.tsx`, the four disaster pages, `ToolFinder.tsx`, rebuilt into `web/app/`; `tools.py` and
