# DepthWizard updates, 15 October 2026

## UI
- Three modes: **Explore** | **Validate & calibrate** | **Disaster tools**. Each mode shows only its own controls.

## SIH26175 requirements
- **Height validation in the app.** Compare our heights with a reference DSM, nDSM or DTM, surveyed points (CSV),
  or NASA ICESat-2 laser data. Reports MAE, RMSE, correlation and bias, with a scatter plot, an error map and a
  split by landscape (urban, sparse, hilly, forested).
- **Ground control point calibration.** Click points of known height, then fit and apply. Also turns PNG/JPG
  heights into absolute ones. Adds a calibrated DSM download.
- **Accuracy measured.**

  | Benchmark | MAE | Correlation r |
  |---|---|---|
  | GAMUS vs LiDAR, all pixels | 4.0 m | 0.66 |
  | GAMUS vs LiDAR, urban | 2.8 m | 0.85 |
  | ICESat-2, Indian urban scenes | 5.3 m | |
  | ICESat-2, Indian sparse scenes | 2.1 m | |
  | ICESat-2, Indian hilly scenes | 6.9 m | |
  | ICESat-2, Indian forested scenes | 10.5 m | |

  Main weakness: trees and tall buildings come out too low.
- **Standalone app.** `Install.bat` (one-time setup) and `DepthWizard.bat` (opens the app). Works offline after
  installation and runs on CPU without an NVIDIA GPU.
- **Documentation.** `README.md`, `docs/USER_GUIDE.md`, `docs/TECHNICAL.md`.

## Disaster tools
- **Connected flooding:** water comes from the scene edges or points you click, not just all low ground.
- **Route finder:** from A to B, on foot or by car. Avoids buildings, steep slopes and deep water.
- **Rain & river timeline:** hour-by-hour flooding from rain (or the real forecast) and river rise. Includes a
  "time until flooded" map.
- **Helipad & relief camp finder:** flat, open, dry sites, with capacity and road access.
