DepthWizard field points

Record GPS positions of places whose height you know (survey marks, benchmarks) or measure, then send them to DepthWizard. In the app: Validate & calibrate → 📍 Control points → Load field-app points.

No GPS here? Type the position
Phone GPS heights are only good to about ±10 m; for calibration prefer a survey-grade GNSS receiver or the known height of a mark. Browsers give GPS only on secure pages (https) or on this computer itself; on a phone over plain http, type the position instead. Points are kept on this phone until they are sent.
