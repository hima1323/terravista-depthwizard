    Calibrate with ground control points

    Click points of known elevation (survey marks, benchmarks, GNSS points) and enter their height in metres above sea level. 1 point fixes the level; 2+ points that differ in height also fix the height scale; 4+ also correct a tilt. Works for PNG/JPG too: they then get absolute heights.

    Line of sight

    What can be seen from a point. 1. Set the heights 2. click Place observers and click the map 3. green = visible, dark = hidden.

    Volume & area

    Draw an outline around a debris pile, stockpile, pond or group of buildings. ✎ Draw outline, click the corners, then ✓ Done.

    Drone flight plan

    1. Draw a path (2D or 3D), or find a route in Disaster tools 2. check the heights 3. export for the drone app.

    Rooftop solar

    Yearly solar energy of every roof. Shading from taller buildings and trees is traced along the sun's path through the year.

    Map overlay (WMS)

    Lay roads, boundaries or land use from a web map service over the scene: OpenStreetMap, ISRO Bhuvan or any other WMS. Needs internet once, then it is saved.

    Mosaic: join scenes

    Joins neighbouring GeoTIFF scenes into one larger scene, from their saved heights (no new model run).

    Building heights from shadows

    Height = shadow length × tan(sun elevation). Take the sun elevation and azimuth from the image's metadata, or compute them from its acquisition time. Assumes a near-vertical view.

    Fix tall objects

    The model tends to under-estimate tall buildings and trees. Scale them using the shadow tool (buildings) or NASA ICESat-2 canopy heights (trees), or set the factors yourself.

    Calibrated error bars

    The ± spread of the model ranks errors but is not in true metres. A validation you ran calibrates it (split conformal): then “± 3.1 m (90 %)” means 9 in 10 true heights lie within it.

    Terrain source (DEM)

    Use your own elevation model instead of Copernicus GLO-30, e.g. ISRO CartoDEM (from Bhuvan) or SRTM. It must cover the whole scene.

    Flowing water (2D)

    Water spreading over time from a breach, river or drain outlet: a 2D shallow-water simulation on the terrain. 1. Place inflow points 2. ▶ Run 3. drag the time slider.

    Evacuation plan

    Sends each building's residents to the nearest relief camp that still has space, along routes that avoid deep water. Uses the camps from 🚁 Relief sites.

    People at risk

    Residents per building ≈ floors × footprint × occupied share ÷ floor space per person, counted for each hazard tool you use.

    Damage: before / after

    1. Open the after-event scene (this one) 2. choose the before image 3. Compare. Buildings that lost height are marked collapsed or damaged.

    Landslide risk (screening)

    Rates each place from terrain slope, land cover and local relief (ratings after BIS IS 14496 Part 2), raised for heavy rain. Not a full hazard zonation: rock type, faults and groundwater are not included.

    Observed flood (satellite)

    Load a real flood map, a Sentinel-1 radar image or any water mask (GeoTIFF), to check the flood scenario against it or fit its water level.
