# DepthWizard updates, 4 November 2026

## Easier to find
- **Toolbar** under the mode tabs: every tool of the current mode, one click away. Click again or **×** to close.
- **🔍 Find a tool (Ctrl K)**: searches all tools in all modes and switches the mode for you. Example: `drone` + Enter.
- Each tool card starts with one line saying what it does and the steps. Same look and colours as before.
- **📄 Report**: the map, the 3D view and all open cards as a printable page (save as PDF).
