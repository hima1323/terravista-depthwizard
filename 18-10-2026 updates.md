# DepthWizard updates, 18 October 2026

## Evaluation redone (protocol v2)
Every reported number now has a 95% confidence interval over tiles or scenes and a baseline measured at the same
points. Each one has a row in `docs/CLAIMS.md`. Reports are in `SynRS3D/demo/benchmark/v2/`.

| Benchmark | MAE (95% CI) | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| GAMUS vs LiDAR, 150 tiles (aerial 0.33 m, zero-shot) | 3.90 m [3.24, 4.66] | flat ground: 5.65 m |
| GAMUS buildings, 1,450 | 3.57 m [3.08, 4.06] | flat ground: 10.54 m |
| Overhead Geopose Atlanta, satellite, 300 tiles | 5.99 m [5.57, 6.43] | published RS3DAda: 5.67 m |
| Overhead Geopose Buenos Aires, satellite, 300 tiles | 1.94 m [1.86, 2.03] | published RS3DAda: 2.01 m |
| India, heights vs ICESat-2 segments, 8 scenes | 4.85 m [3.04, 6.69] | height 0: 8.25 m |
| India, terrain vs held-out ICESat-2 ground, 5 scenes | 3.73 m [1.44, 6.69] | no levelling: 3.86 m |

- **GAMUS is aerial imagery, not satellite.** The satellite claim now rests on Overhead Geopose, where the published
  RS3DAda numbers reproduce.
- **The Indian numbers no longer check the terrain against the photons that levelled it.** Levelling removes the bias
  but not the typical error.
- **Worse than before, and stated:**
  - our absolute DSM does not beat the raw Copernicus DSM at laser footprints (9.14 vs 8.06 m);
  - in forest every DEM is about 20 m off, and ours is the worst;
  - the old "r = 0.98 in hills" was the hillside; with the terrain plane removed, r is 0.43.
- **The main weakness is unchanged:** tall buildings and trees come out too low, by 15–18 m above 20 m on
  every test set.

## Uncertainty
- **The flip-spread map is renamed "Consistency score".** It does not rank errors on tall objects.
- **Building blocks show a 90% interval** calibrated on GAMUS LiDAR.
  - It covers 83–97% of blocks on a held-out city, and 71% on Indian roofs.
  - It misses most buildings over 10 m, and the viewer says so.

## Terrain and ICESat-2
- **No laser levelling from a single pass.** This fixes the Silent Valley 47 m shift, which came from one line along a
  valley floor that the 30 m DEMs fill in. `TERRAIN_VERSION` is now 3, so cached scenes rebuild.
- **New "Held-out passes" table** in the ICESat-2 panel, with a note when levelling was refused.
- **Tried and not shipped:**
  - FABDEM as the default terrain: no base won every test;
  - GEDI-densified levelling: no gain;
  - isotonic height recalibration: Philadelphia got worse;
  - input resampling: no gain.

## Deck and docs
- **Deck v4** (`deck_work/TerraVista_DepthWizard_SIH2026_v4.pptx`, built by `build_v4.py`) describes what is built.
  - The model is RS3DAda with released weights and no fine-tuning.
  - DINOv3 / CHMv2, ONNX and Tauri are marked as planned.
  - The flood CSI 0.79 is labelled a scenario test.
  - Every number comes from `docs/CLAIMS.md`.
- **README and TECHNICAL** have new accuracy sections and known limitations. The USER_GUIDE has the new uncertainty
  and validation wording.
- **`docs/DATA_REQUEST_cartosat.md`** is a draft request to the PS owner / NRSC for a Cartosat scene and CartoDEM over
  Delhi. It has not been sent.

## Not yet validated
- Cartosat and CartoDEM;
- leaf-on tree canopy;
- interval coverage outside US cities;
- floor counts, GCP accuracy and processing times;
- the disaster tools, apart from one flood scenario.
